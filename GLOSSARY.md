# Cemiyet

Cemiyet is a personal reading and literary community platform. Its language distinguishes books and their published editions from ownership, reading journeys, and public participation.

## Language

### Readers and credited identities

**Reader account**:
A reader's Cemiyet identity, distinct from the public names credited on books and from a publisher organization.
_Avoid_: Author identity, publisher organization

**Reader profile**:
The reader-facing presentation of an account, including its username, display name, and chosen public information.
_Avoid_: Entire personal library

**Author identity**:
A public identity or pseudonym credited as an author of a Work, whether or not anyone has claimed it through a Reader account.
_Avoid_: Login account, verified account owner

**Author claim**:
A verified association between a Reader account and an Author identity.
_Avoid_: Ownership of reader reviews

**Publisher record**:
The bibliographic identity of a publisher or imprint associated with an Edition, independently of participation in Cemiyet.
_Avoid_: Publisher organization

**Publisher organization**:
A publishing organization represented in Cemiyet by authorized managing accounts.
_Avoid_: A second personal login, publisher record

### Books and catalog contributions

**Work**:
A book-level literary or illustrated creation considered independently of its published editions.
_Avoid_: Edition, owned copy, book when the distinction matters

**Adaptation**:
A distinct Work derived from another Work through a substantial creative rewriting or transformation.
_Avoid_: Ordinary translation, edition of the original by default

**Book series**:
A grouping of related Works, with reading or publication order where one is defined.
_Avoid_: A single Work, adaptation relation

**Edition**:
A particular published version of a Work, distinguished by its publication, language, content version, and product format.
_Avoid_: Individual copy, unchanged reprint

**Owned copy**:
A reader's separately tracked instance of a book when they choose detailed ownership tracking.
_Avoid_: ISBN, edition, library entry

**Provisional book**:
A privately usable book record that has not yet been matched to an accepted catalog identity.
_Avoid_: Verified public catalog entry

**Catalog contribution**:
A proposal to add, identify, or correct bibliographic information, supported by identifiers or source evidence.
_Avoid_: A private reading record, automatic public catalog publication

**Catalog merge**:
The reviewed reconciliation of duplicate catalog identities into a canonical identity while preserving their valid associations and histories.
_Avoid_: Deleting a reader's history

### Personal library and reading

**Library entry**:
A reader's saved personal association with a Work or Provisional book, with ownership information when the reader supplies it.
_Avoid_: Proof of ownership, reading journey

**Reading intention**:
A reader's expressed wish to read a book, including a future reread.
_Avoid_: Started reading journey

**Reading journey**:
One reader's distinct attempt to read a book, including its own progress and outcome.
_Avoid_: Reading session, a Work's single global reading status

**Progress record**:
An observation of a reader's position in a Reading journey, expressed in an applicable unit.
_Avoid_: Proof of completion, time spent reading

**Personal note**:
A reader's own annotation about a Work or Reading journey, distinct from a published Review or Activity.
_Avoid_: Public review by default

**Quotation**:
An excerpt recorded with its source and any known location information.
_Avoid_: The reader's own review text

**Custom list**:
A reader-defined grouping of books, independent of ownership and reading outcomes.
_Avoid_: An exclusive reading status

**Loan**:
A reader's record of lending a book or tracked copy to another person.
_Avoid_: Transfer of ownership, automatic update of the recipient's library

### Evaluation and sharing

**Current rating**:
A reader's current public evaluation of a Work, distinct from historical impressions recorded for individual Reading journeys.
_Avoid_: An extra vote for every reread or copy

**Review**:
Reader-authored commentary about a Work, optionally associated with an Edition or Reading journey.
_Avoid_: Required rating, proof of completed reading

**Spoiler**:
A disclosure that may reveal an experience the reader wants to discover through the work itself.

**Sharing audience**:
The people entitled to access a deliberately shared item, distinct from its discoverability or the author's follower count.
_Avoid_: Implicit permission from following

**Follow**:
A one-way choice to receive another reader's accessible public activity.
_Avoid_: Friendship, private-data access

**Published activity**:
A reader's deliberately shared reading-related update or content, distinct from an unshared personal record.
_Avoid_: Every internal change to a reading journey

### Clubs and shared reading

**Club**:
A membership-based community for shared reading and discussion, optionally associated with a physical location.
_Avoid_: Follower list

**Club membership**:
A reader's participation in a Club with an assigned role and membership state.
_Avoid_: Permission to inspect personal reading history

**Shared reading**:
A Club's organized reading of a Work, distinct from every participant's personal Reading journey.
_Avoid_: Automatically started personal reading

**Gathering**:
An online or in-person Club event with a time, place or joining link, and attendance intentions.
_Avoid_: A reading journey, paid ticket by default

### Goals and recognition

**Personal reading goal**:
A reader's chosen reading target measured over an identified scope or period.
_Avoid_: Mandatory competition

**Reading challenge**:
An optional shared reading target or campaign with defined participation and counting rules.
_Avoid_: A club membership, a leaderboard by default

**Contribution badge**:
Nonfinancial recognition of an approved useful contribution.
_Avoid_: Spendable currency, reward for submission volume

**Reading summary**:
A reader-specific account of their reading over a defined scope or period, distinct from platform usage analytics.
_Avoid_: Operational telemetry

### Moderation and account lifecycle

**Moderation case**:
A recorded matter concerning content or conduct, considered within a defined Club or platform scope.
_Avoid_: Automatic punishment from report volume

**Posting restriction**:
A limitation on a reader's publication or interaction capabilities within a defined scope.
_Avoid_: Deletion of the personal library

**Club expulsion**:
The ending of a person's membership in one Club.
_Avoid_: Automatic platform-wide ban

**Account erasure**:
The process of ending a Reader account and removing the personal data associated with it.
_Avoid_: Posting restriction, deleting independent bibliographic facts

### Data portability

**Import batch**:
A group of externally supplied library or reading records reviewed and processed as one transfer into Cemiyet.
_Avoid_: A new reading journey for every retried row

**Export package**:
A reader-requested portable representation of their personal library and reading records.
_Avoid_: Public publication of personal records
